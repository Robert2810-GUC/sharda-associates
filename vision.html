What We Believe In

OUR VISION STATEMENT

"To be Uttar Pradesh's most trusted name in government construction and residential development — building communities, not just structures."

At Sharda Associates, we have always believed that a building is more than its materials. It is a commitment to the people who will work within it, live in it, and be served by it. Every government building we construct improves public service delivery. Every row house we build gives a family their home. That is the weight of responsibility we carry into every project.

Our long-term vision is to grow Sharda Associates into a pan-Uttar Pradesh construction brand — known not just for the scale of its work, but for the consistency of its quality, the integrity of its dealings, and the depth of its contribution to the communities of Lucknow and beyond. We want to be the contractor government departments trust by default and the builder families choose with confidence.

🎯

OUR MISSION

To deliver government-grade construction quality on every project — regardless of scale. To treat every client's budget as our own, every deadline as a commitment, and every structure as our signature. We exist to build things that last and relationships that endure.

🔭

OUR VISION

To become Uttar Pradesh's premier construction company by 2030 — expanding from Lucknow to every major district in UP. To be the name synonymous with government infrastructure excellence and quality residential development across the state.

Where We're Going

OUR GOALS FOR 2030

Six strategic commitments that guide our growth and define our ambition for the decade ahead.

🏛️

Expand Government Project Portfolio

Secure contracts across all 18 districts of Lucknow division — moving beyond Jankipuram to build government infrastructure at a regional scale.

🏘️

Build 200+ Row Houses by 2030

Develop 200 or more quality row house units in Lucknow by 2030, creating complete gated colonies that offer affordable quality living for middle-income families.

📜

ISO Certification Upgrade

Achieve ISO 9001:2015 quality management certification to further standardize our construction processes and qualify for larger government tenders across India.

📱

Digital Project Tracking for Clients

Launch a client portal where project stakeholders can track progress in real time — viewing milestone completion, photo updates, and cost utilization reports live.

🗺️

Pan-UP Presence by 2027

Establish operational presence in at least 5 UP districts beyond Lucknow by 2027 — with dedicated site teams, local supplier networks, and regional offices.

🌱

Sustainable Construction Practices

Integrate green building principles into our construction methodology — using eco-friendly materials, reducing site waste, and adopting energy-efficient design approaches.
