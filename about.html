DHANESH

"We don't just build structures. We build trust, we build communities, we build the future of Uttar Pradesh."

— Dhanesh Agrawal, Founder & Director

Dhanesh Agrawal began his journey with nothing more than a deep passion for construction and an unshakeable belief in honest work. Coming from humble beginnings in Lucknow, he worked his way up through the construction industry — learning every aspect of the trade from the ground up, from laying foundations to managing complex multi-storey government projects.

In 2005, he founded Sharda Associates with a clear vision: to bring professional, transparent, and high-quality construction services to Lucknow and the surrounding districts of Uttar Pradesh. What started as a small contracting firm has grown into one of the region's most trusted names in government construction and residential development.

Under his leadership, Sharda Associates secured its first government contract in 2010 — a milestone that opened the door to a steady stream of projects with government bodies, municipal corporations, and district administrations. Today, with over 500 completed projects and 350+ satisfied clients, Dhanesh continues to lead from the front — on-site, hands-on, and committed to quality at every stage.

Company Story

OUR JOURNEY

2005
Sharda Associates Founded

Dhanesh Agrawal establishes Sharda Associates in Lucknow with a small team and a big vision — to build honestly and build to last.

2010
First Government Contract

A landmark moment: securing our first official government contract for a public building project. This opened the doors to a new era of institutional construction work.

2015
Row House Division Launched

Expanding into residential development, Sharda Associates launches its row house construction division, building complete colonies for families across Lucknow.

2022
500+ Projects Milestone

Sharda Associates crosses the 500-project mark — a testament to 17 years of consistent quality, reliable execution, and earned client trust across UP.
